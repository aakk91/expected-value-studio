Keep the scorer in the shot.

Keep one player visible and cover the other faces before sharing a team video from your iPhone.

How to cover everyone except one player

  1. Choose the video. Nobody scans it on your iPhone and covers the faces it detects.
  2. Tap the scorer’s face chip to keep that person visible. Leave the other players covered.
  3. Replay the whole clip, including people entering at the edges. Add a manual box over anything the scan missed, then save the covered copy to Photos.
