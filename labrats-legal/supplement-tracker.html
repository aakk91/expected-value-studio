A supplement tracker that shows your results too

Tick what you took, play your daily check-in, and LabRats lines the two up. After a few weeks each supplement has its own before and after, game by game.

By Amine Kara, maker of LabRats · Updated 20 November 2026

Log a supplement in two taps

  1. Lab tab, What you took, Edit, Add a product. Name it, add an amount if you like.
  2. Each day, tap the chip for what you took. If today matches the last day you logged, tap Same as yesterday.
  3. Tap a product's name to see the days you took it, fill in a missed day, or edit the amount.

Your daily check-in does the measuring

The check-in on Today is a few short games, usually a few minutes in all. The games rotate across speed, memory, reasoning, spatial and multitasking, so every skill comes back regularly. Nothing is asked before you play: you can tick your supplements before or after.

See before and after

Each product sheet compares the days you logged it with the days you didn't, since your first log. It shows each game on its own, with its own unit. A difference only shows as clear when it stands out from your normal day-to-day spread. Most first weeks read Not clear yet, which is the honest answer.

Tip. If you take something every single day, the comparison has nothing to work with. Test this on the product sheet plans a few days without it.

Free, private, yours

Logging, comparisons and personal experiments are free. Your Lab stays on your phone; account sync is optional and you can export your data. LabRats gives no dose advice and makes no health claims.
