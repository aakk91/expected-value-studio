Support

Write to applabrat@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version from Settings, and what you did before the problem. Please don't send passwords, sign-in codes or card details.

A purchase isn't showing

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that paid. Check you are signed in to the right LabRats account first.

Cancel or manage a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, LabRats. Apple handles billing, renewal and refunds; refunds go through Report a Problem.

Sync and offline play

Games and the Lab work offline. With sync on, anything saved offline uploads when you're back online. Don't delete the app to fix a problem while you have unsynced records; they would be lost.

Switching accounts on one phone

Records never move between accounts. If you sign in with another account, the app tells you what hasn't synced yet and asks before erasing this phone's copy.

Get a copy of your data

In Settings, Request a copy of my data covers what is stored in your account. Settings also has export options for the records on your phone.

Delete your account

Settings, Account, Delete my account. This deletes your account data held by LabRats. It does not cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that separately.

Report a player or content

Use Report or Block on their profile, or email the pseudonym and what happened.

Ads

Ad privacy choices are in Settings. Premium has no ads. Your Lab, notes, sleep, mood and game results are never used for ad targeting.
