Log your nootropics next to your game results

LabRats is an iPhone app where you tick the nootropics you took today and play a few short games. Over the weeks it sets the days you took something against the days you didn't, game by game.

By Amine Kara, maker of LabRats · Updated 20 November 2026

Add a nootropic in the app

  1. Open the Lab tab. Under What you took, tap Edit, then Add a product.
  2. Type the name as you know it, for example L-theanine or Bacopa. Amount, unit and time are optional.
  3. From then on it sits in What you took as a chip. Tap it on the days you take it. Same as yesterday ticks the whole list in one tap.
  4. Forgot a day? Open the product, then tick the missed days in Days you took it.

The name is free text. LabRats keeps no product list and suggests no doses.

What the comparison looks like

Open a product and choose See before and after. For each game you played on both kinds of day, the app shows your usual result on days you logged it and on days you didn't. Only the days you ticked count as taken, so a forgotten day is never assumed.

If you take something every day, there is nothing to compare it against. The app says so and suggests a few days without it, or a planned experiment.

When to run an experiment instead

A log is good for keeping a record. If you have one clear question, Test this on the product sheet starts a personal experiment with set periods with and without it. Its result reads one measurement at a time: reaction time in milliseconds, memory span, errors under distraction.

What a result can and cannot say

A better week can come from sleep, practice or a quieter office. The app only reports differences it can tell apart from your usual day-to-day spread, and it never says a product caused them. It is not medical advice. Talk to a clinician before changing anything you take for a condition.
