Attention and focus tests that show what slowed you down

Attention games in LabRats put a distraction in front of you and measure what it costs. Stroop shows a word in a clashing colour. Flanker surrounds the arrow you need with arrows pointing elsewhere. Go/No-Go asks you to hold back.

By Amine Kara, maker of LabRats · Updated 27 November 2026

The games

  • Stroop. Tap the ink colour, not the word. The result says how many milliseconds the clashing words cost you per answer.
  • Flanker. Point the way the middle arrow points.
  • Go / No-Go. Tap for every letter except X. Taps on X count against you.
  • Shapes & colors. The rule switches between shape and colour, and you switch with it.
  • Find the odd one and Symbol match. Visual search and matching under time.

Speed and errors belong together

You can always go faster by being careless. Every attention result keeps both sides: correct answers and correct answer time. When a later run is faster but makes more mistakes, the app calls it a trade-off rather than an improvement.

Repeat it under the same conditions

Distractions around you count as much as the ones on screen. Play at similar times, in a similar place, with notifications off when you can. In the Lab you can note a Stressed or Late night day, and those tags show up next to your results.
