A peptide journal with your games beside it

LabRats keeps a private log of what you take, peptides included, next to short cognitive games you play during the day. Your entries and your results sit on the same calendar.

By Amine Kara, maker of LabRats · Updated 27 November 2026

Add a peptide entry

  1. In the Lab tab, under What you took, tap Edit and Add a product.
  2. Enter the name you use. Amount and unit are optional and kept exactly as you type them.
  3. Tick it on the days you take it. Untick removes that day, with Undo.
  4. Under About the day you can note sleep, energy, mood, tags such as Sick or Late night, and a free note.

What you can record

FieldWhere
Name, amount, unit, timeLab, What you took
Days taken, including missed days filled in laterThe product sheet, Days you took it
Sleep, energy, moodLab, About the day
Your own measurements, tags and a noteLab, About the day
Reaction, memory, attention resultsAny game you play that day

Reading it later

The product sheet shows the days you logged it and how your games went on those days compared with the others. For a sharper answer, a personal experiment sets fixed periods with and without it.

The app does not recommend peptides, doses or cycles and does not judge safety. Everything in your Lab stays on your phone unless you turn on account sync.
