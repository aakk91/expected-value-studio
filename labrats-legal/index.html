Three things, one app

A daily check-in to measure, a Lab to log your days, and Ranked when you want to compete.

Check in each day

A few short games, a few minutes in all. They rotate through speed, memory, reasoning, spatial and multitasking so every skill comes back. No questions before you play.

  • Each result in its own unit: milliseconds, items, correct answers
  • Compared with your own usual, not with a population average
  • A streak and three test tubes on Today to keep you coming back

Log your day in the Lab

Tap what you took today: supplements, nootropics, peptides, coffee, anything you name. Add sleep, energy, mood, a tag or a note if you like.

  • Each product gets a before and after from your own games
  • Test this turns a question into a planned experiment
  • Stays on your phone unless you turn on sync

Compete in Ranked

Press Play and the app deals a game and an opponent who played the same items. Win, lose or draw. Your Elo moves per skill, and each month the best players race for the Top 100.

  • 20 rated games, an Elo for each skill
  • Tiers from Bronze to Diamond
  • Five free duels a day, unlimited with Premium

30 games, each measuring one thing

From a 30-second reaction test to a three-minute puzzle. Every game saves a result you can read, and many have a guided lesson.

Five skills

Speed, memory, reasoning, spatial and multitasking, plus games for scene memory, map reading, pitch and spotting AI images. Your profile on the You tab shows all five on one chart.

Honest about what it can tell you

It describes, it doesn't diagnose

LabRats measures how you played a game. It is not a medical device, doesn't recommend products or doses, and never says that something you took caused a change.

It says when it doesn't know

A difference only reads as clear when it stands out from your normal day-to-day spread. Otherwise you'll see Not clear yet.
