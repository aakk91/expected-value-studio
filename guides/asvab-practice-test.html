What is on the CAT-ASVAB?

The CAT-ASVAB has ten subtests. The table gives the number of scored questions and the time limit that the official ASVAB site publishes for each one, without tryout questions. Auto Information and Shop Information are two separate subtests on the computer and are reported together as one Auto and Shop score.

SubtestScored questionsMinutesWhat it asks
General Science (GS)1512Life, earth and physical science
Arithmetic Reasoning (AR)1555Word problems solved with arithmetic
Word Knowledge (WK)159The meaning of a word, alone or in a sentence
Paragraph Comprehension (PC)1027Questions answered from a short paragraph
Mathematics Knowledge (MK)1531Algebra and geometry
Electronics Information (EI)1510Circuits, current, components
Auto Information (AI)107How vehicles work and are maintained
Shop Information (SI)106Tools and workshop practice
Mechanical Comprehension (MC)1522Levers, gears, pulleys, forces
Assembling Objects (AO)1518How separate pieces fit together

When tryout questions are added to a subtest, it has 15 more questions and a longer time limit. They are not scored and you cannot tell them apart, so answer every question as if it counts.

ASVAB practice questions with worked answers

Yes. Try an original question from each of the four AFQT subtests, then a mechanical reasoning question. Open each answer after you have made your choice.

Arithmetic Reasoning: a recruit runs 3 miles in 27 minutes. At the same pace, how long does a 5-mile run take?

45 minutes. 27 ÷ 3 = 9 minutes per mile, and 5 × 9 = 45. The usual trap is to add the 2 extra miles to 27 without converting them to minutes.

Word Knowledge: “abundant” most nearly means: scarce, plentiful, costly or distant?

Plentiful. Abundant means existing in large quantity. Scarce is its opposite, which is why it appears among the options.

Paragraph Comprehension. Read this passage: “The base library moved its afternoon delivery to the morning. Several requests now arrive after the van leaves. Staff hold those requests for the next scheduled trip.”

Which conclusion does the passage support? A. Fewer people use the library. B. Some requests wait for a later trip. C. The van makes two trips each day. D. Staff refuse requests after noon.

B. The last sentence explains what happens to requests that miss the van. The passage gives no figures for library use, no trip frequency and no deadline for accepting requests.

Mathematics Knowledge: if 3x − 7 = 11, what is x?

x = 6. Add 7 to both sides: 3x = 18. Divide by 3: x = 6. Check: 3 × 6 − 7 = 11.

Mechanical Comprehension: a 12-tooth gear drives a 36-tooth gear. When the small gear makes 6 turns, how many turns does the large gear make?

2 turns. The same number of teeth passes the contact point on both gears: 6 × 12 = 72 teeth, and 72 ÷ 36 = 2. The larger gear always turns more slowly, and in the opposite direction.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are on the CAT-ASVAB?

The CAT-ASVAB has 135 scored questions across ten subtests: 15 each in General Science, Arithmetic Reasoning, Word Knowledge, Mathematics Knowledge, Electronics Information, Mechanical Comprehension and Assembling Objects, and 10 each in Paragraph Comprehension, Auto Information and Shop Information. Unscored tryout questions can be added to two to four subtests.

Which ASVAB subtests count for the AFQT?

Four: Arithmetic Reasoning, Mathematics Knowledge, Word Knowledge and Paragraph Comprehension. The AFQT is reported as a percentile and determines eligibility to enlist; the other subtests feed the line scores used for job qualification.

Can I use a calculator on the ASVAB?

No. The official ASVAB site states that calculators are not allowed, because the test measures whether you can apply principles to solve problems. Practise Arithmetic Reasoning and Mathematics Knowledge on paper.
