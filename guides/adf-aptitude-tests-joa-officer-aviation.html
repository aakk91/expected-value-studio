What happens before and after the ADF aptitude test?

You receive the online JOA invitation after starting your ADF application. The familiarisation questions let you learn the controls before the timed assessment begins. Take the time to check how to select or enter an answer and how to move between questions. Use the instructions in your invitation for equipment and test-day arrangements.

The JOA measures how you reason with unfamiliar information. ADF Careers says there is no need to study specific subject matter and recommends its own guide for familiarisation. During the actual assessment, work independently. Afterwards, your Careers Coach can discuss the roles open to you in light of your results, eligibility and interests.

Can I try free numerical, verbal and abstract JOA questions?

Work out each answer before opening its explanation. The first two problems both involve numbers but call for different operations: one uses a rate and the other a changing pattern.

Numerical reasoning. A pump fills three identical 18-litre containers in six minutes. At that rate, how much water does it deliver in ten minutes? Choose 30, 54, 90 or 180 litres.

90 litres. Three containers hold 54 litres. Divide by six minutes to get 9 litres per minute, then multiply by ten. The 54-litre option gives the volume at the six-minute mark.

Number pattern. Which number comes next in 8, 11, 17, 20, 26, …? Choose 27, 29, 30 or 32.

29. The successive increases are 3, 6, 3 and 6. The next increase is 3, taking 26 to 29. Choosing 32 repeats the last increase twice in a row.

Verbal reasoning. A compass indicates direction. Which instrument has the same relationship to temperature: a gauge of speed, a thermometer, a clock or a scale?

A thermometer. Look for an instrument and the particular quantity it measures. A compass indicates direction, just as a thermometer indicates temperature. All four choices are measuring instruments, so that fact alone does not distinguish the answer.

Abstract reasoning. Read this sequence of arrows from left to right: ↑, ↗, →, ↘, … . Would the next arrow point left, up-right, down or down-left?

Down (↓). Each arrow rotates clockwise by one eighth of a turn. From down-right the next position is straight down. Down-left would require two steps. The same rule applies across all four visible arrows.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to study for the ADF JOA?

ADF Careers says the assessment does not require subject-specific study. Start with the official guide and its familiarisation questions. The original worked examples here are optional.

How long is the Job Opportunities Assessment?

ADF Careers gives 20 minutes for 51 questions after the instructions and familiarisation. The candidate guide says most people do not complete every question.
