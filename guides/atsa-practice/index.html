KEYBOARD RADAR SAMPLE · ABOUT 2 MINUTES

Keep moving targets apart

Four waves of numbered targets fly straight across the scope. Press a target’s number to remove it before two of them collide, press Space when no conflict remains, and in the last two waves answer sums with A–D while traffic keeps moving. Mouse clicks work too.

This is the same scenario and scoring engine as the radar section of the ATSA programme in the iPhone app.
