Can I try Civil Service-style questions with answers?

Passage. The enquiry desk opens at 09:00 on weekdays. Staff answer written enquiries within five working days. Enquiries about pensions are passed to a separate team, which sets its own deadlines.

Verbal: “The enquiry desk opens at 08:00 on Mondays.” True, False or Cannot say?

False. Monday is a weekday, and the passage says the desk opens at 09:00 on weekdays. The statement is contradicted.

Verbal: “The enquiry desk is open on Saturdays.” True, False or Cannot say?

Cannot say. The passage gives the weekday opening time and says nothing about weekends. Absence of information is not a contradiction.

Verbal: “A written enquiry about pensions is answered within five working days.” True, False or Cannot say?

Cannot say. Pension enquiries go to a separate team with its own deadlines, which the passage does not give. They could be shorter or longer than five days.

Numerical: a team handled 1,200 cases in 2025 and 1,380 in 2026. What was the percentage increase?

15%. The increase is 180 cases. Divide by the starting value: 180 ÷ 1,200 = 0.15. Dividing by 1,380 instead answers a different question.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Civil Service Verbal and Numerical Tests timed?

No. GOV.UK says the tests are not timed and that most people take between 15 and 45 minutes for each one. They adapt to your performance, and you cannot skip a question or go back.

What does “Cannot say” mean in the Civil Service Verbal Test?

It means the passage neither supports nor contradicts the statement. Choose False only when the passage says the opposite; choose Cannot say when the passage is silent or incomplete on the point.

Can I use a calculator in the Civil Service Numerical Test?

Yes. GOV.UK suggests having a calculator, rough paper and a pen to hand. The difficulty is in choosing the right figures from the graph or table.
